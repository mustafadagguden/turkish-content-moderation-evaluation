# Annotation policy and versioning

- **Safe:** ordinary conversation, questions, compliments, disagreement, or criticism without explicit insults, degradation, harassment, or threats. Sarcasm alone does not establish harmfulness.
- **Harmful:** explicit insults, degrading language, harassment, or threats. Under this study's policy, explicit insults aimed at someone's comment or work can also qualify.
- **Ambiguous:** both harmful and harmless readings are reasonably supported, and missing context prevents a reliable choice. Mere possibility of sarcasm or uncertainty about the policy is insufficient.

Requests to stop contact are not automatically threats. Threats may be implicit; the speaker need not explicitly promise to cause harm. Distinguish a warning about consequences from a threat where context allows.

Policy boundaries remain around shaming, hostile commands, and non-insulting sarcasm. The policy was clarified during human review; it was not independently validated. Examples such as ID 6 show residual inconsistency between some frozen labels and the clarified rule.
